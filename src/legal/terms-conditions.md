## 5. Acceptable use

Do not use Whomie to:

- **Post fake or misleading listings.** Only list a room that exists, that you are allowed to rent out or share, and with honest photos, rent, deposit and dates.
- **Harass people.** No threats, insults, stalking, unwanted sexual messages, or contacting someone again after they have blocked you.
- **Discriminate.** Do not post content that demeans or excludes people because of their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sexual orientation or gender. Stating your household preferences with the app's own options, such as a gender preference for a shared home, is allowed where the law allows it.
- **Scam people.** Do not ask for money, deposits, bank details, passwords or identity documents under false pretences. Do not send links to phishing or malware.
- **Advertise or spam.** Whomie is for finding rooms and flatmates. Do not send bulk messages or advertise unrelated products or services.
- **Share other people's data.** Do not post anyone's address, phone number or other personal data without their permission.
- **Post illegal or harmful content**, including sexual content, violence, or content that encourages crime.
- **Misuse the service.** Do not scrape data, use bots, try to get around a block, break into accounts or systems, or interfere with how Whomie works.

**Payments.** Whomie does not handle payments between users. Any rent, deposit or other money you agree with another user, for example under a lease, is a matter between you and that user.

<a id="safety"></a>
## 6. Meeting people and viewing rooms safely

**Whomie is not a party to any lease, sublease or other agreement between users.** We do not own, rent out or manage any room, and we are not an estate agent. We do not check users' identities or listings. You decide who you talk to, who you meet and what you agree to.

Some simple rules help:

- Meet for the first time in a public place, or bring a friend to a viewing.
- See the room in person before you pay anything or sign anything.
- Never send a deposit or rent to someone you have not met, or for a room you have not seen.
- Check that the person has the right to rent out the room, and read any contract before you sign it.
- Keep your conversations in the app until you trust the other person.
- If something feels wrong, stop, and report it to us.

If you are in danger, contact the police or emergency services first.

<a id="reporting-and-blocking"></a>
## 7. Reporting and blocking

You can report a user or a room listing in the app, and you can block a user.

- **Blocking** hides you and the blocked user from each other's people and chat lists. Neither of you can start a new chat with the other. Messages you have already exchanged remain.
- **Reporting** sends us the report. We review reports and may remove content, warn users, or suspend or close accounts.

**We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.** We remove such content, and we suspend or remove users who post it or abuse others.

You can also write to admin@whomie.com about anything that worries you.
